# Action-extreme — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow tracking dolly behind the protagonist
- low-angle hero shot during a leap, kick, or strike
- locked-off medium two-shot for the confrontation beat
- single Dutch tilt as tension peaks

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a single leap from one rooftop to another
- one strike (kick / punch / sword cut), connecting cleanly
- one fast vault over an obstacle
- a slow rise from a knee at the close

## Pacing
- One peak action per clip; resolve cleanly within 8s.
- Hold the locked frame after the peak for at least 1 second.
- Sub-drop in audio aligned with the peak.

## Avoid
- Multiple peak actions in a single clip.
- Score swelling under every move.
- Slow-motion abuse on every strike.
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashes.
