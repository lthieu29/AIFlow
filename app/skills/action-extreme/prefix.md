# Action-extreme skill prefix

## Style and lighting
High-octane third-person action. Hard rim light from a low sun or
practical lamp; deep shadow falloff; muzzle-flash or spark accents
allowed but kept naturalistic. Anamorphic lens flare permitted on the
peak beat. Kinetic photoreal grade.

## Camera framing
Slow tracking dolly behind the protagonist, low-angle hero shot during
the leap or strike, locked-off medium two-shot for the confrontation.
Allowed motion: tracking, slow push-in, low-angle hero, single Dutch
tilt for tension. Shot framing favours wide and medium.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named athlete-protagonist (parkour runner, stunt
driver, fighter) in three-quarter rear angle. Hold wardrobe and rig
across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: hard surface impacts (foot, fist, board edge), rushing wind,
deep cinematic sub-drop on the peak beat, sustained low brass.
Optional Dialogue: a single short challenge line.

## Constraints specific to action-extreme
No real identifiable face. No real branded sponsor gear. No live
ammunition shown. No explicit injury, gore, blood spray.
