# Action-sports-pov — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- locked POV with natural rig bob during running / cycling
- locked POV leaning with the body during cornering
- locked POV dropping with the descent during ski / MTB
- POV breath sway suggested by tiny vertical wobble

## Approved subject micro-motions
- hand tightens grip on the handlebar
- ski poles plant once, push, release
- board edge cuts a single carved arc
- rope or harness tightens for a single beat

## Pacing
- One sustained motion per clip (descent / corner / drop).
- Resolve the motion within 8 seconds.
- Audio breath sync with rig sway.

## Avoid
- Drone shots, third-person cutaways.
- Strobe / epilepsy effects.
- Cuts that break POV.
