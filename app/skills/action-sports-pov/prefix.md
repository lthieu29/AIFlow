# Action-sports-pov skill prefix

## Style and lighting
First-person POV sports photography. Sun-flash daylight key, neutral
midday balance, naturalistic outdoor light. Photoreal, no stylised colour
grade. Helmet-mount or chest-mount perspective with motion blur on edges.

## Camera framing
Locked POV from a wearable mount with natural rig sway. Allowed motion is
the rig motion itself: bob with running, lean with cornering, drop with
descent. Shot framing locks at extreme wide due to the lens character.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named first-person activity (mountain biking, surfing,
skiing, parkour, BMX). The hands or board edges enter frame consistently
to confirm POV; the world rushes by.

## Audio
Audio: rushing wind, breath through helmet, gear sound (chain, board,
ski edges, footsteps), distant ambience of the location. Optional
Dialogue: a single short reaction line.

## Constraints specific to action-sports-pov
No real branded helmet visible. No third-person cuts. No bright
artificial colour grade. No real identifiable persons.
