# Action-wuxia — Character template

## Default archetypes
- Wandering swordsman: white silk robes, conical hat, jade-pommel sword.
- Sect master: deep crimson layered robes, long beard, fan or staff.
- Female disciple: long black hair, pale silk robes, paired daggers.

## Identity anchors
- Robe layers and silk colours stable across the beat block.
- Weapon held the same way across the choreography.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Modern accessories (phones, sunglasses).
- Branded clothing. Real-world brand markings on weapons.
