# Action-wuxia — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow tracking dolly across a misted plateau
- locked-off wide of the duel
- single tilt-up to follow a robe leap
- extreme close-up on the sword draw

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a sword half-drawn, then locked
- one robe sleeve flutters in slow motion
- a single leap to a higher branch / rooftop
- two arcs of the blade meet, then hold

## Pacing
- One peak choreography per clip.
- Slow-motion reserved for the sword arc itself, never for entry.
- Brass gong on stance lock; silence holds after.

## Avoid
- Multi-warrior chaotic mass action.
- Score swelling under every line.
- Slow-motion abuse across every move.
