# Action-wuxia skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Wuxia and xianxia martial arts cinema. Misted bamboo grove, ink-wash
mountain ridge, golden temple courtyard. Soft volumetric daylight key
through fog, gentle backlit silk robes, brass lantern accents at night.

## Camera framing
Slow tracking dolly during a chase across rooftops, locked-off wide
of the mountain duel, single tilt-up to follow a robe leap. Allowed
shot framing: wide for the duel, medium for the stance, extreme close-up
for the sword draw.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named warrior (wandering swordsman in white silk robes,
sect master in deep red, female disciple with long black hair). Hold
the same wardrobe and weapon across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: brass gongs on stance lock, distant pipa or guzheng, silk
fluttering in wind, the single ring of a sword drawn, mountain wind.
Optional Dialogue: a single classical-style line.

## Constraints specific to wuxia
No real identifiable face. No modern slang in dialogue. No anachronistic
props (phones, glasses, sneakers). No anime / cartoon stylisation. No
explicit gore (stylised combat only).
