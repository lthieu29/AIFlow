# Action-wuxia — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip wuxia duel)

| Beat       | Goal                                             | Typical shot |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| arrival    | wide of the mountain plateau in mist             | wide static |
| stance     | medium of warrior, sword half-drawn              | medium static |
| clash      | sword arcs meet, slow-motion silk fluttering     | medium two-shot |
| breath     | landed stance, soft brass gong, silence holds    | wide static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named environment per block (bamboo grove, mountain ridge,
  temple courtyard, snow plateau).
- Mist or wind in every shot to soften silk and steel.
- Gong / pipa cue placed once per block, not on every beat.

## Continuity across the block

- Same warrior, same wardrobe, same weapon.
- Same hour-of-day and atmospheric mist intensity.
