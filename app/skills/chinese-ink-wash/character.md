# Chinese-ink-wash — Character template

## Default archetypes
- A solitary scholar in a hanfu-style robe, brush in hand at a stone desk.
- A poet by a window, looking out at distant peaks.
- A boatman on a misted river, conical hat, pole oar.

## Identity anchors
- Robe layers and brush implements stable across the block.
- Same misted scenery vantage; do not jump terrains mid-block.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Modern accessories visible.
- Branded clothing. Real-world brand markings.
