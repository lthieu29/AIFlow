# Chinese-ink-wash — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow dolly across a misted mountain ridge
- locked-off wide of a brush at work on rice paper
- single rack focus from brush tip to distant peak
- single tilt-up to follow a calligraphy stroke into the sky

## Approved subject motions
- a brush draws a single ink stroke on rice paper
- a robe sleeve flutters once in mountain wind
- a boatman's oar dips in misted water once
- a single bird crosses the negative space

## Pacing
- One brush gesture or environmental beat per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the close-of-stroke frame for at least 2 seconds.
- Guzheng phrase continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cuts, drone yaw spins.
- Multiple brush strokes overlapping in one shot.
- Score swelling under every line.
