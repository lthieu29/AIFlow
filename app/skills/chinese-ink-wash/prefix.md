# Chinese-ink-wash skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Chinese ink-wash painting style (shui-mo) translated into photoreal
motion. Soft volumetric daylight diffused through mountain mist;
brush-stroke negative space; minimal palette of black ink, rice-paper
beige, single accent of jade or vermilion.

## Camera framing
Slow dolly across a misted mountain ridge; locked-off wide of a
calligraphy hand at work; single rack focus from a brush tip to a
distant peak. Shot framing leans extreme wide for landscape and
extreme close-up for the brush-on-paper gesture.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named scholar archetype (solitary scholar in hanfu,
poet by a window, boatman on a misted river). Hold the same robe and
brush across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: a guzheng or pipa phrase; soft mountain wind; the gentle scrape
of brush against rice paper; distant temple bell. Dialogue: a single
classical-style line, formal phrasing.

## Constraints specific to ink-wash
No real identifiable face. No modern slang in dialogue. No anachronistic
props (mobile phones, neon, plastic). No anime / cartoon stylisation.
No real-world Chinese branding.
