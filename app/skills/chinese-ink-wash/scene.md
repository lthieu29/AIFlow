# Chinese-ink-wash — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip ink-wash narrative)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| ridge      | wide of misted mountain ridge, brush negative space | extreme wide static |
| brush      | extreme close-up of brush on rice paper           | extreme close-up |
| poet       | medium of scholar / poet at desk                  | medium static |
| flight     | single bird crosses the negative space, mist holds | wide static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Brush-stroke negative space dominant in every shot.
- Single named environment per block (mountain ridge, river, courtyard).
- Mist or wind in every shot to soften silk and ink.

## Continuity across the block

- Same scholar, same wardrobe, same brush.
- Same hour-of-day and atmospheric mist intensity.
