# Cinematic Action — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for high-energy cinematic action beats. Camera motion
> is allowed for this skill (manifest sets `camera_lock: false`). Use at most
> 2–3 micro-motions per scene and resolve them within the 8s clip.

## Camera motions

- fast tracking follow behind running subject
- low-angle push-in on hero standing ground
- Dutch tilt hold for psychological tension
- rapid whip pan to reveal threat
- slow-motion pull-back to show scale of destruction
- tight handheld shake during impact

## Subject micro-motions

- explosive sprint → slide → cover
- draw weapon in one fluid motion
- dodge → counter-strike → land
- slow turn to face the camera — determined expression
- stagger from impact → recover stance
- reach for cover, press against wall, scan

## Slow-motion moments (impact beats)

- bullet casing ejecting in mid-air
- shattered glass suspended at peak
- the decisive punch or kick landing
- protagonist leaping between rooftops

## Pacing

- High energy: tight cuts, fast motion, minimal holds
- Slow-motion reserved for peak impact moments only
- Aftermath beats: slower, let the weight land
- Match motion energy to beat type — approach builds, impact peaks, aftermath settles

## Avoid

- Gratuitous gore or explicit injury detail
- Comedic or slapstick movement
- Anime-style speed lines or cartoon physics
- Camera shake so extreme it obscures the subject
