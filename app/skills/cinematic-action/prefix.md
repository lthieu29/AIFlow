# Cinematic Action skill prefix

## Style and lighting
High-octane cinematic action film. Dramatic high-contrast key with
deep shadows and punchy rim light; muzzle flash, spark, or
practical-source accents allowed but kept naturalistic. Photoreal
live-action blockbuster aesthetic with anamorphic lens character.

## Camera framing
Dynamic — fast tracking follow behind the protagonist, low-angle
hero shots during stand-grounds and leaps, locked-off medium two-shot
for confrontations, single Dutch tilt for psychological tension.
Slow-motion reserved for the impact beat only.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named characters defined in ``character.md`` — hero,
antagonist, ally, civilian — each with a hard identity anchor (build,
hair, signature wardrobe). Original fictional likeness only.

## Audio
Dialogue: short, terse, impact-driven — one or two lines maximum per
beat. Audio: hard surface impacts (foot, fist, blade), rushing wind,
deep cinematic sub-drop on the peak beat, sustained low brass cue.

## Constraints specific to cinematic-action
No real identifiable persons. No anime, illustration, or cartoon
stylisation. No gore, no explicit injury detail, no live ammunition
shown — violence stays cinematic and non-gratuitous. No real branded
sponsor gear or weapon markings.
