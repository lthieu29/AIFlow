# Cinematic-drama — Character template

## Default archetypes
- The lead: a single named adult with one defining wardrobe element
  (cardigan, apron, suit jacket). Photoreal but never a real face.
- The confidante: a friend or family member, dressed in the same palette.
- A child: present in the background, playing quietly with a single prop.

## Identity anchors
- Wardrobe and accessory continuity through the beat block.
- Hair and makeup natural, period-consistent.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded clothing logos. Glamorised violence.
