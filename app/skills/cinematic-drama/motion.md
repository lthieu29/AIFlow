# Cinematic-drama — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow dolly-in on a dialogue beat (one foot per second)
- single rack focus between two seated characters
- locked-off medium two-shot at a table
- a quiet reveal: dolly out from face to room

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a single hand sets a teacup down
- a tear forms but does not fall
- the lead exhales once, eyes closing
- a child runs across the back of the frame, crossing once

## Pacing
- One primary motion per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the silent beat for 1.5-2 seconds before the cut.
- Match dolly speed to the breath of the dialogue.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, drone shots.
- Score swelling under every line.
- Overlapping micro-motions that compete for attention.
