# Cinematic-drama skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Prestige cinematic drama. Naturalistic motivated lighting — soft window
key, practical lamp fill, blue-hour glow through curtains. Muted earth
tones (dust, oat, deep green) with a single warm accent on the lead.
Subtle film grain and soft halation on highlights.

## Camera framing
Slow dolly-in on emotional beats, single rack focus between two characters,
or locked-off medium two-shot at the table. Shot framing favours medium
close-up for dialogue and extreme close-up for reaction.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the lead actor: name them with one trait (mid-40s mother in a
faded green cardigan, retired teacher in wire-rim glasses). Hold wardrobe
and demeanour across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: one or two short lines per clip, low natural delivery.
Audio: distant kitchen sounds, a clock ticking, a dog snoring, a single
piano phrase under the silence. Rarely score-driven.

## Constraints specific to drama
No melodramatic camera moves. No glossy CG light. No anime / cartoon. No
explicit violence or NSFW. No real identifiable persons.
