# Cinematic-noir — Character template

## Default archetypes
- The PI: long trench coat, fedora, cigarette, gloved hand. No visible
  full face — rim-lit silhouette or three-quarter profile only.
- The suspect: sharp suit, loosened tie, shadowed eyes. Standing under a
  single overhead lamp.
- The fixer: behind a typewriter, sleeves rolled, suspenders showing.

## Identity anchors
- Costume silhouette stable across the beat block (same coat, same hat).
- Cigarette smoke trail consistent direction, light source unchanged.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded clothing. Daylight backgrounds.
- More than two characters in frame at once.
