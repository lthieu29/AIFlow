# Cinematic-noir — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow tracking shot down a wet street (dolly along rails)
- dolly out from a tight three-quarter profile to wide of the lamp
- rack focus from rain droplets in foreground to silhouette behind
- locked-off two-shot for the confrontation beat

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a lighter clicks once, ember glows on the face
- smoke exhaled in a single deliberate plume
- the trench coat turns once, never twice
- one figure walks away in a single steady stride

## Pacing
- One primary motion per clip; never combine tracking with rack focus.
- Hold the locked moment for at least 2 seconds before cutting.
- Rain rhythm is constant; do not vary intensity across the block.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, drone shots.
- Multiple light sources flickering simultaneously.
- Bright daylight breaks across consecutive scenes.
