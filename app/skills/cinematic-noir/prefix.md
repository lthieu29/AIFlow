# Cinematic-noir skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Film noir: high-contrast cinematic black-and-white with selective colour
on rain-slicked streets and neon spill. Hard low-key key light from a
single window or street lamp; deep shadows; chiaroscuro carved faces.
Anamorphic lens character; subtle film grain.

## Camera framing
Tracking shot down a narrow wet street, or a locked-off medium of a lone
figure under a single street lamp. Allowed motion: slow tracking, dolly
out from face to street, or rack focus from foreground rain to figure.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on one named archetype per scene: the PI in a long trench coat,
the suspect under the lamp, the fixer behind a typewriter. Hold wardrobe
and silhouette across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: terse, two-line maximum, world-weary tone.
Audio: rain on pavement, distant jazz piano from a smoky bar, a muffled
trumpet, the click of a lighter, a slow saxophone phrase.

## Constraints specific to noir
No daylight. No bright primary colours. No anime / cartoon stylisation.
No real identifiable face for the protagonist.
