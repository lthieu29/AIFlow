# Cinematic-noir — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip noir teaser)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| street     | wide of wet street, neon reflected in puddles     | wide tracking |
| figure     | lone silhouette under a single street lamp        | medium tracking |
| confront   | two figures face off in low-key light             | medium static, two-shot |
| exit       | trench coat walks away into rain                  | wide static, lock |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named light source per shot (window, street lamp, neon sign).
- Smoke or rain visible in every shot to soften highlights.
- Rule of thirds: hero figure on a vertical third, light source opposite.
- Negative space reserved on bottom for caption bar in post (not burned).

## Continuity across the block

- Same street, same lighting cue, same rain intensity.
- When the location changes (interior to street), rotate the lamp source
  but keep the costume and cigarette continuity.
