# Cinematic-period — Character template

## Default archetypes
- A single named protagonist in period wardrobe (corseted dress, tweed
  travelling suit, starched collar).
- A confidante or servant in matching period dress, kept in soft focus.
- The villain: same era, sharper silhouette, dark wardrobe.

## Identity anchors
- Wardrobe layered with period accessories (gloves, brooch, pocket watch).
- Hair styled to era; no modern cuts.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Modern accessories visible. Branded clothing.
- Anachronisms (phones, neon, plastic).
