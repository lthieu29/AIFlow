# Cinematic-period — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow tracking dolly through a corridor
- locked-off medium two-shot at a dining table
- single tilt-up to reveal a chandelier
- carriage approach: slow tracking from gate to door

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a candle flame wavers once
- a letter opens with a single tear of paper
- a brooch catches a gas-lamp gleam
- a glove pulls off in one slow gesture

## Pacing
- One move per clip; ease.
- Hold the lock for at least 2 seconds.
- Match dolly to the chosen quartet phrase.

## Avoid
- Drone shots, whip pans, handheld shake.
- Slow-motion abuse.
- Swelling score under every line.
