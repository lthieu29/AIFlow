# Cinematic-period skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Period cinematic drama set between 1900 and 1940. Candlelit and gas-lamp
interiors, soft daylight through tall windows. Wax-candle warm key, brass
or amber rim, deep-amber falloff. Faint smoke and dust visible in the
air. Anamorphic lens character with film grain.

## Camera framing
Slow tracking dolly through a long corridor, locked-off medium two-shot
at a dining table, or a single tilt-up to reveal a chandelier. Shot
framing favours wide establishing and medium close-up.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on one named protagonist with period wardrobe (a corseted dress
in deep green, a tweed travelling suit, a starched collar). Hold the
costume across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: formal, archaic phrasing in measured tempo.
Audio: a hearth fire crackling, distant carriage wheels on cobble, a
single dogmatic clock ticking, soft string quartet phrase.

## Constraints specific to period
No anachronistic props (mobile phones, neon signs, modern logos). No
modern slang in dialogue. No real identifiable persons or actors.
