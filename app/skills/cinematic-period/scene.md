# Cinematic-period — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip period teaser)

| Beat       | Goal                                            | Typical shot |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| arrival    | wide of carriage at gates, gas lamps lit        | wide static |
| salon      | medium 2-shot at dining table, chandelier above | medium 2-shot |
| reveal     | extreme close-up on a letter, candle wavers     | extreme close-up |
| exit       | tracking dolly through long corridor            | wide tracking |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Single named period light per shot (candle / gas lamp / hearth / window).
- Period-appropriate props only.
- Smoke or dust visible in every interior shot.

## Continuity across the block

- Same costume, same hour, same set dressing.
- When location changes (carriage → salon), justify travel cleanly.
