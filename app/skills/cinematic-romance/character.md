# Cinematic-romance — Character template

## Default archetypes
- The leads: one couple, named simply (man in a navy jacket, woman in a
  linen dress). Photoreal, no real identifiable face; obscured by hat,
  shadow, or three-quarter angle.
- The witness: a kind older bystander on a park bench, present but not
  central.

## Identity anchors
- Wardrobe and hairstyles held across the beat block.
- Both leads kept under the same lighting block; do not split them
  across opposite light directions in consecutive scenes.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded clothing. Explicit physical contact.
- Audience cheering, roses falling from above, melodramatic stage tropes.
