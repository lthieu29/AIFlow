# Cinematic-romance — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow push-in on a close-up during dialogue
- slow pull-back on a reaction beat
- locked-off two-shot at table / bench
- single rack focus between the two leads

## Approved subject micro-motions
- two hands meet on a table, hold for a beat
- a single laugh fades, eyes lower
- coat collar turns once in a breeze
- a smile breaks slowly, no abrupt cuts

## Pacing
- One motion per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the silent beat for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Match push-in speed to the line read.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, drone shots.
- Slow-motion abuse on every kiss.
- Score swelling that drowns the dialogue.
