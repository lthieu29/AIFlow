# Cinematic-romance skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Cinematic romance. Warm soft golden-hour key with halation on highlights,
side-light from windows, subtle ambient haze. Bokeh-rich shallow depth of
field. Vintage warm tone curve, gentle film grain.

## Camera framing
Slow push-in on close-ups during dialogue, slow pull-back on reactions,
locked-off two-shot at the table or park bench. Shot framing favours
medium close-up and extreme close-up of hands or eyes.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named couple: name each (man in a navy jacket, woman in a
linen dress). Hold wardrobe and hairstyles across the beat block. Keep
both in the same lighting block.

## Audio
Dialogue: short, tender, one-line exchanges. Pauses respected.
Audio: distant fountain water, soft acoustic guitar, autumn leaves in
breeze, a single laugh that fades.

## Constraints specific to romance
No melodramatic camera. No NSFW. No explicit physical contact beyond a
hand hold or a single glance. No real identifiable persons.
