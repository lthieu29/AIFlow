# Cinematic-romance — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip romance excerpt)

| Beat       | Goal                                          | Typical shot |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------|
| meet       | wide of café terrace, two figures cross       | wide static |
| spark      | medium two-shot at table, eye contact         | medium two-shot |
| confession | extreme close-up on a single tear / smile     | extreme close-up |
| afterglow  | pull-back to wide, golden hour fades          | wide dolly out |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named light source per shot (window, lamp, candle, sun).
- Soft halation and warm grain on every highlight.
- Bokeh background in mediums and close-ups.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same hour of day, same prop set.
- When the location changes, justify it (walk to the next set), do not
  jump arbitrarily.
