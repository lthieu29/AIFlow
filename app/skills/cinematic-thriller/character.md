# Cinematic-thriller — Character template

## Default archetypes
- Protagonist: a single adult, damp coat, no face revealed; back of head,
  hands, or three-quarter rear silhouette only.
- Watcher: silhouette in a doorway or at the end of a corridor.
- Operator: hands at a glowing terminal, sleeves rolled up.

## Identity anchors
- Protagonist's coat / hair-line silhouette stable across the block.
- Watcher kept in soft focus or cut off by the door frame.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded clothing. Explicit violence shown on
  screen. More than one watcher visible at once.
