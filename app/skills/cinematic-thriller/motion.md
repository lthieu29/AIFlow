# Cinematic-thriller — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow tracking dolly behind the protagonist down a corridor
- locked-off extreme close-up on hands at a desk or steering wheel
- single rack focus from a phone screen to a watcher in the doorway
- a tilt-up that follows a slow elevator door opening

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a key turns in a lock once, locks click in sequence
- a phone vibrates, screen flashes a notification
- a coat collar lifts, water drips from the brim
- a watcher enters the doorway and freezes

## Pacing
- One move per clip; let it breathe.
- Hold the locked beat for at least 1.5 seconds before cutting.
- Synth drone tempo unchanged across the block.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, shaky cam.
- Multiple watchers entering simultaneously.
- Score swelling under every line.
