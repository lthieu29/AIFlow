# Cinematic-thriller skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Cold cinematic thriller. Hard contrasted top-key light, deep navy and
graphite shadows, single fluorescent or sodium-vapour practical source.
Anamorphic flare on highlights, rich black levels, subtle grain.

## Camera framing
Slow tracking dolly behind the protagonist down a corridor; locked-off
extreme close-up on hands at a desk; single rack focus from a phone screen
to a watcher in the doorway. Shot framing leans tight to keep tension.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on one named protagonist (woman in a damp coat, IT analyst at a
glowing terminal). The watcher is always partly hidden — silhouette or
doorway frame. Hold wardrobe across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: clipped one-line exchanges, low and fast.
Audio: a sustained synth drone, ticking clock, distant siren, the click
of a mechanical keyboard. No music swell; tension carried by ambient.

## Constraints specific to thriller
No daylight. No bright primaries. No explicit violence shown — implied
only. No real identifiable face. No anime / cartoon stylisation.
