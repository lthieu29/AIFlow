# Cinematic-thriller — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip thriller teaser)

| Beat       | Goal                                            | Typical shot |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| arrival    | wide of the corridor / lobby, fluorescent buzz  | wide tracking |
| watcher    | rack focus from phone screen to a doorway       | medium static |
| confront   | tight close on hands at a desk, drawer opens    | extreme close-up |
| exit       | tracking dolly behind protagonist, door closes  | wide tracking |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named practical light per shot.
- Anamorphic flare on the brightest highlight per frame.
- Negative space at the top of frame for a watcher to appear later.

## Continuity across the block

- Same corridor, same flicker pattern of the fluorescent light.
- Watcher silhouette stays partial across the entire block.
