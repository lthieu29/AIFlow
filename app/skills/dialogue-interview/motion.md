# Dialogue-interview — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- locked-off medium close-up of the guest
- very slow push-in during a key answer
- locked-off wide of the room as ambient cutaway

## Approved subject micro-motions
- guest sips tea once
- a thoughtful nod
- a pen tapped once on paper
- a slow exhale before answering

## Pacing
- One push-in per block, on the most emotional question.
- Hold the answer beat for natural breath length.
- Audio room tone continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cutaways.
- Multi-camera rapid switching.
- Score under the dialogue.
