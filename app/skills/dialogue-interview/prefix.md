# Dialogue-interview skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Editorial long-form interview. Soft window key from the right, gentle
fill from a paper bounce on the left, subtle warm practical lamp in the
background. Photoreal, prestige documentary grade.

## Camera framing
Two-camera setup feel: wide frame of the room and a tighter medium of
the speaker. Allowed motion: very slow push-in during the most
emotional answer, otherwise locked. Shot framing favours medium and
medium close-up on the speaker.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named guest (a chef in chef-whites, an academic in
soft tweed, an artist in a paint-flecked smock). Hold the same wardrobe,
hairstyle, and chair across all clips of the interview block.

## Audio
Dialogue: prepared answers, longer-form, measured tempo.
Audio: soft room tone, distant kitchen / studio ambience, gentle pen
scratch, occasional page turn. No music.

## Constraints specific to interview
No real identifiable face. No real branded backdrops or sponsor
products. No teleprompter visible. No hot-mic interruption SFX.
