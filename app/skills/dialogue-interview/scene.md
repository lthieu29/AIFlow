# Dialogue-interview — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip interview excerpt)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| open       | wide of the room, guest settles                   | wide static |
| answer     | medium close-up, slow push-in on emotional answer | medium close + push |
| reflect    | medium static, longer pause held                  | medium static |
| close      | wide static, guest looks down, breath             | wide static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Single named light source per shot (window / lamp / both balanced).
- Negative space at top of frame for caption or pull-quote in post.
- Background props say something quiet about the guest (one book, one
  tool, one plant); no clutter.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same chair, same backdrop, same lighting block.
- Audio room tone continuous across cuts.
