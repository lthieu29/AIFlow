# Dialogue-podcast-clip — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- absolutely locked-off (booth feel)

## Approved subject micro-motions
- single nod
- one sip from a coffee mug
- one hand gesture during emphasis
- mouth movement matched to the audio caption

## Pacing
- The caption beat aligns to the audio segment.
- One subject motion per clip; held cleanly.
- Audio waveform pulse subtle under the caption box.

## Avoid
- Camera motion of any kind.
- Multi-speaker interruption cuts.
- Score under the dialogue.
