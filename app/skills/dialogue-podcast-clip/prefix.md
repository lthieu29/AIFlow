# Dialogue-podcast-clip skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Podcast booth highlight clip. Warm key light on the speaker, deep dark
booth backdrop, single practical lamp behind for rim. Photoreal,
narrative-grade. Vertical-friendly framing for caption space.

## Camera framing
Absolutely locked-off booth shot — one-shot of the speaker or two-shot
of host and guest. Negative space reserved for the caption box (audio
caption added in post; do not burn into the frame). Shot framing
medium / medium close-up.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the speaker(s): name them with archetype (host in horn-rim
glasses, guest in casual blazer). Hold wardrobe and mic placement
across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: short punchy quote — one to two sentences max per clip.
Audio: clean studio room tone, no environmental ambience. The audio
clip is the hero; visuals serve it.

## Constraints specific to podcast-clip
No real podcast network branding. No real branded microphones. No
burned-in caption (added in post). No camera motion at all.
