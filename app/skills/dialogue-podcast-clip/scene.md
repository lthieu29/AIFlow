# Dialogue-podcast-clip — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 1-clip podcast highlight)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| quote      | speaker delivers the punchy line, mouth synced    | medium static, locked |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Speaker(s) on rule of thirds, top 30% of frame reserved for caption.
- Subtle audio-waveform line at the bottom edge.
- Booth backdrop kept consistent.

## Continuity across the block

- Same speaker, same mic placement, same lighting.
- If chained into a 2-3 clip highlight reel, keep all of the above.
