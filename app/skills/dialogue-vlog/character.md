# Dialogue-vlog — Character template

## Default archetype
- One named vlogger: mid-20s creator at a desk in cream sweater,
  weekend dad in flannel at the kitchen counter, art student in
  headphones in a sunlit studio.
- Single subject in frame; no co-host.

## Identity anchors
- Wardrobe and hairstyle stable across all clips of the block.
- Same desk / kitchen / studio backdrop and props.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Real branded merch in frame.
- Burned-in caption text. Reaction faces of frozen shock.
