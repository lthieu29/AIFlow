# Dialogue-vlog — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- light handheld breathing on the confessional close-up
- single tilt-down to reveal a desk prop
- locked-off wide for the establishing shot

## Approved subject micro-motions
- subject mouths a casual greeting once
- subject sips coffee or tea once
- a single pen tap or notebook flip
- subject leans forward once for emphasis

## Pacing
- One natural-breath beat per clip; do not rush.
- Hold the closing takeaway frame for at least 1 second.
- Audio room tone continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast B-roll cuts.
- Reaction-face freeze frames.
- Score under the dialogue.
