# Dialogue-vlog skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Confessional handheld vlog aesthetic. Soft window or ring-light key,
warm domestic colour grade, gentle film grain. Photoreal short-form
look.

## Camera framing
Handheld breathing on a confessional close-up; tilt-down on a desk
prop reveal; locked-off wide for an establishing room shot. Shot
framing leans medium close-up to extreme close-up, eye-level.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named vlogger (mid-20s creator at a desk in cream
sweater, weekend dad in flannel, art student in headphones). Hold
wardrobe and prop set across the beat block.

## Audio
Dialogue: chatty, casual, two-line sentences, slight ums for realism.
Audio: domestic ambience (kettle, distant traffic, fan), no music
track unless the script asks.

## Constraints specific to vlog
No real identifiable face. No branded gear / merch in frame. No
on-screen burned text overlays. No clickbait reaction faces.
