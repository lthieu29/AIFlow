# Dialogue-vlog — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip vlog excerpt)

| Beat       | Goal                                         | Typical shot |
|------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------|
| open       | confessional close-up, casual hello          | medium close-up |
| story      | tilt-down to a desk prop, voice continues    | extreme close-up |
| close      | back to confessional, single takeaway line   | medium close-up |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named cosy backdrop per block (desk + plant, kitchen + window).
- Negative space at top of frame for caption space (do not burn).
- Eye-line direct to the lens during confessionals.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same prop set, same lighting block.
- Audio room tone continuous across cuts.
