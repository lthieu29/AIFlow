# Ecommerce-beauty — Character template

> Most beauty product clips do NOT show a face. When a model is required
> the description below is the default; the adapter may override it.

## Default model archetype
- Wrist, palm, fingertip, lips, or single eye only — never a full face for
  ID-protection.
- Skin tone neutral, well-hydrated, no visible pores at macro range.
- Single ungloved hand or single isolated body part per scene.
- Manicure neutral or matte to avoid clashing with the product palette.

## Identity anchors
- Skin texture stable across a beat (no sudden tan / pore changes).
- Wrist or hand always returns to the same angle for product-on-skin shots.

## Forbidden
- No real identifiable face. No tattoos that suggest a real person. No
  branded jewellery on the model.
