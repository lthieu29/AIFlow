# Ecommerce-beauty — Motion vocabulary

> Default is locked-off static. The vocabulary below lists the only motions
> allowed for this skill; pick at most one motion per 8-second clip.

## Approved camera motions
- slow orbit around the bottle (10-20 degrees, ease in/out)
- single rack focus from cap to label
- slow macro push-in toward the dropper or swatch
- single tilt-down to reveal a label as the product rotates

## Approved subject micro-motions
- droplet falls from a dropper, lands cleanly, no splash
- finger swatches a swipe of cream or balm in one stroke
- powder puff lifts off a palette in a small soft cloud
- bottle rotates 30-45 degrees on a turntable, label catches light

## Pacing
- One primary motion per clip; resolve cleanly within 8 seconds.
- Ease-in / ease-out on every camera or subject motion.
- Hold the final frame for at least 1 second so the hero shot reads.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, handheld shake.
- Multiple simultaneous motions (orbit + rack focus together).
- Liquid splashing across the lens or violent ASMR pours.
