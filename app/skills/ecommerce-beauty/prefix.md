# Ecommerce-beauty skill prefix

## Style and lighting
High-end glossy beauty photography. Soft butterfly key light from a large
overhead diffuser, optional gentle gold rim light to lift the bottle edge.
No harsh shadows, no overexposed highlights.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static macro frame on the hero product. Allowed motion vocabulary
is limited to a slow orbit or a single rack focus, picked once per scene.
Frame the bottle, tube, or palette so the label is legible and the cap
catches a soft specular highlight.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor identity on the product first (named bottle shape, colour, finish);
then on the hand, swatch, or droplet that interacts with it. Keep the same
prop set across consecutive scenes within the same beat.

## Audio
Audio: low-mix glassware clinks, soft fabric brushes, ASMR-grade dropper
clicks. No music unless the scene asks for it.

## Constraints specific to beauty
No fingerprints on glass, no dusty packaging, no melting product, no
allergic-reaction or before/after medical claims framed as proof.
