# Ecommerce-beauty — Scene template

> Each scene maps to one ~8s Veo3 clip. Locked-off static frame by default.

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip ad)

| Beat       | Goal                                  | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------|--------------|
| hook       | reveal the product against backdrop   | macro hero   |
| feature    | one named feature (texture, dropper)  | extreme close-up |
| application| product touches skin / palm / wrist   | macro with hand |
| payoff     | final hero with brand-friendly mood   | macro hero, locked |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Negative-space backdrop (cream / blush / dark velvet) chosen once per
  beat block; do not switch mid-block.
- Bottle, tube, or palette label legible if it appears.
- One specular highlight on the cap or rim per shot — not multiple.
- Liquid, cream, or powder textures crisp and natural; no plastic-looking
  CG texture.

## Continuity across the block

- Same backdrop colour, same key-light direction, same macro depth-of-field
  bokeh shape across consecutive scenes.
- When the beat changes (e.g. application → payoff), the lighting may shift
  but the product and surface remain the same.
