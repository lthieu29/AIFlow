# Ecommerce-fashion — Character template

> Template used by the adapter to describe a model/presenter shown with the
> product. Keep faces neutral and non-identifiable (no real persons).

## Identity anchor (hard pin — reused across every scene)

- **Build**: {{BUILD}}  (e.g. "slim, average height")
- **Hair**: {{HAIR}}  (e.g. "shoulder-length dark brown, straight")
- **Wardrobe**: {{WARDROBE}}  (the product being featured + neutral basics)
- **Expression**: neutral, closed-mouth, no teeth visible
- **Skin/makeup**: natural, soft matte, editorial-clean

## Framing defaults

- Knees-up framing when the product is in shot
- Subject engaged with the camera, calm and confident
- Hands relaxed; avoid covering the product

## Hard constraints

- No real identifiable persons
- No brand logos other than the featured product
- Identity must stay consistent across all scenes (same model, same wardrobe)
- Frontal, neutral reference frame is the canonical anchor for i2v continuity
