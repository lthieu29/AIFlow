# Ecommerce-fashion — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for "sweet", controlled product motion. Combine at
> most 2–3 micro-motions per scene. Camera stays locked-off (see `_base`).

## Subject micro-motions

- half-step → glance → hair-tuck
- slow turn to show garment back
- smooth product lift toward camera
- gentle fabric sway / drape settle
- relaxed shoulder shift, weight change
- hand-smooth over fabric to show texture

## Product-only motions (no model)

- slow Ken Burns push-in on detail
- 15–20° turntable rotation
- soft parallax drift across the product
- rack focus from logo to texture

## Pacing

- One primary action per clip; resolve it within the 8s
- Ease-in / ease-out — no abrupt starts or stops
- Match motion energy to the beat (hook = brighter, CTA = settled)

## Avoid

- Fast whip motion, jump cuts, strobing
- Camera shake, handheld bob
- More than one major action per clip
