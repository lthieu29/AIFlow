# Ecommerce-fashion skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Editorial fashion photography. Photoreal magazine grade. Soft even key
light from a large diffuser, gentle paper-bounce fill, slight warm tone
curve and fine film grain. No harsh shadows, no overexposed highlights.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static knees-up frame when the model is wearing the product;
locked-off macro for fabric / texture detail. Allowed motion vocabulary
is limited to a single rack focus or a slow Ken Burns push-in (see
``motion.md``). Shot framing is medium for full-look, tight macro for
texture.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named model archetype with neutral closed-mouth
expression, hands relaxed and not covering the product. The model
wears the featured product plus neutral basics; same wardrobe across
the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: low room tone, soft fabric brushes, a single fashion-shoot
shutter click, optional muted lounge piano. Dialogue is rare; if used,
keep to a one-line tagline.

## Constraints specific to fashion
No real identifiable persons. No brand logos other than the featured
product. No teeth visible in the model's expression. No cluttered
props that compete with the garment.
