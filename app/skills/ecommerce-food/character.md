# Ecommerce-food — Character template

> Template used by the adapter when a presenter or hand model is shown
> interacting with the food or beverage product. Keep faces neutral and
> non-identifiable (no real persons). Hands should look clean and natural.

## Identity anchor (hard pin — reused across every scene)

- **Hands**: {{HANDS}}  (e.g. "clean, natural skin tone, no nail polish")
- **Wardrobe**: {{WARDROBE}}  (e.g. "neutral linen apron, minimal accessories")
- **Expression**: calm, inviting, no forced smile
- **Skin/makeup**: natural, minimal, editorial-clean

## Framing defaults

- Hands-in-frame when demonstrating product use (pouring, plating, unwrapping)
- Face cropped out or shown from chin-down to keep focus on the product
- Props kept minimal — one or two complementary items maximum

## Hard constraints

- No real identifiable persons
- No brand logos other than the featured product
- Hands and props must stay consistent across all scenes
- Food must always look fresh, appetising, and in-focus
