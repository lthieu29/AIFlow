# Ecommerce-food — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for appetising, controlled food and beverage motion.
> Combine at most 2–3 micro-motions per scene. Camera stays locked-off (see `_base`).

## Product micro-motions

- slow pour of liquid into glass — show colour and clarity
- steam rising gently from hot dish or beverage
- condensation droplets forming on cold drink surface
- slow reveal of cross-section (cutting, breaking, unwrapping)
- gentle rotation of plated dish to show full presentation
- sprinkle of garnish (herbs, spice, powder) falling onto product

## Hands-in-frame motions

- smooth lift of product toward camera
- deliberate pour or scoop action
- careful plating — placing garnish with precision
- hand wrapping around warm cup or cold glass

## Product-only motions (no hands)

- slow Ken Burns push-in on texture detail
- soft parallax drift across the hero product
- rack focus from packaging label to food texture
- 15–20° turntable rotation of packaged product

## Pacing

- One primary action per clip; resolve it within the 8s
- Ease-in / ease-out — no abrupt starts or stops
- Match motion energy to the beat (hook = dynamic pour, CTA = settled close-up)

## Avoid

- Fast whip motion, jump cuts, strobing
- Camera shake, handheld bob
- More than one major action per clip
- Motion that makes food look unappetising (splashing mess, spills)
