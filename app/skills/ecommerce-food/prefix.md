# Ecommerce-food skill prefix

Appetising food and beverage product photography. Warm natural window light with soft fill.
Rich warm colour palette — amber, cream, terracotta, and deep green accents. Sharp focus on
the hero product. Overhead or 45-degree hero angle, tight crop. Locked-off static frame.
Garnishes and props arranged to enhance appetite appeal without cluttering the composition.
Steam, condensation, or fresh texture details visible where appropriate. No real persons.
