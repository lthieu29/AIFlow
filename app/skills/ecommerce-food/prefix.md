# Ecommerce-food skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Appetising food and beverage product photography. Warm natural
window light with soft paper-bounce fill; rich warm colour grade.
Photoreal, magazine-grade detail with steam, condensation, or fresh
texture visible where the dish allows.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static frame at overhead or 45-degree hero angle. Allowed
motion vocabulary: slow Ken Burns push-in on a texture detail, soft
parallax drift across the hero plate, rack focus from packaging
label to food texture, gentle 15–20 degree turntable rotation of
packaged product.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named hero dish or beverage; if a presenter is shown,
hands-only or chin-down framing (see ``character.md``). Surface and
prop set held constant across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: a single deliberate pour, soft sizzle, plate-down click, fork
scrape, low café ambience, optional mellow lounge piano. Dialogue is
rare; if used, a single short appetite-led tagline.

## Constraints specific to ecommerce-food
No real identifiable persons. No real branded packaging beyond the
featured product. Food must always look fresh, never spoiled or
mouldy. No splashing mess that makes the dish unappetising.
