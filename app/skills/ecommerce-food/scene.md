# Ecommerce-food — Scene template

> Template the adapter fills per shot. Each scene maps to one ~8s Veo3 clip.

## Shot skeleton

- **Beat**: {{BEAT}}  (hook / feature / proof / CTA)
- **Subject**: {{SUBJECT}}  (product alone, or hands interacting with product)
- **Action**: {{ACTION}}  (single clear action — see motion.md vocabulary)
- **Background**: {{LOCATION}}  (kitchen counter, rustic wood table, marble surface, outdoor café)
- **Framing**: overhead or 45-degree hero angle; tight crop on product detail

## Beat conventions

| Beat | Goal | Typical duration |
|------|------|------------------|
| hook | grab attention — steam, pour, or reveal | 8s |
| feature | show product detail, texture, ingredients | 8s |
| proof | demonstrate use — eating, drinking, cooking | 8s |
| CTA | call to action, price anchor, packaging close-up | 6–8s |

## Composition rules

- One product focus per scene — no clutter
- Locked-off static frame (camera lock inherited from `_base`)
- Leave headroom for lower-third / product-card overlays
- Garnishes and props enhance appetite appeal without obscuring the product
- Keep continuity: same surface, lighting, and props as adjacent scenes
