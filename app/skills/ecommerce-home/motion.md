# Ecommerce-home — Motion vocabulary

> Default is locked-off static; one motion per 8s clip max.

## Approved camera motions
- slow dolly forward into a styled corner (1-2 metres)
- single tilt-up to reveal a tall lamp or shelving piece
- gentle handheld breathing only when the scene needs warmth (rare)
- top-down static for ceramics, textiles, books

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a hand sets a mug down once on the table
- a linen throw settles after one gentle pull
- steam rises softly from a teacup, no violent burst
- a door closes once with a soft click in the background

## Pacing
- One primary motion per clip; ease-in / ease-out.
- Hold the final frame for at least 1 second so the styled set reads.

## Avoid
- Sweeping pans, racing tracking shots.
- Hand entering and leaving the frame multiple times.
- Pet animals running through the shot (separate skill).
