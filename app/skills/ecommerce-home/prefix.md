# Ecommerce-home skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Cosy interior product photography. Soft window key light at golden hour
through sheer curtains; warm tungsten practical lamp adds a second source.
Overcast diffuser fill keeps shadows gentle. No harsh contrast.

## Camera framing
Default is locked-off static at chest height for furniture, eye-level for
desk objects, or top-down for ceramics. Allowed motion: slow dolly forward
into the styled corner, or a single tilt-up to reveal a tall lamp.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named product (sofa, ceramic vase, linen throw) and its
immediate set dressing (one book, one mug, one trailing plant). Hold the
same set across consecutive scenes within a beat block.

## Audio
Audio: low-mix interior ambience — distant traffic through window, soft
fabric brushes, a single ceramic tap. No music unless the scene asks.

## Constraints specific to home
No clutter, no dirty surfaces, no peeling paint, no construction tools, no
visible price tags or barcode stickers on the product.
