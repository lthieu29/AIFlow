# Ecommerce-jewelry — Character template

> Most jewellery clips are pure product shots. When a body part is shown
> the description below applies; otherwise the field is left empty.

## Default model archetype
- Wrist, hand, ear, or neckline only. No full face for ID-protection.
- Skin tone neutral, well-cared, no visible scars or tattoos.
- Single hand or single ear per scene; no twins or mirror reflections.

## Identity anchors
- The piece sits in a fixed orientation across consecutive shots.
- The same finger / lobe / collarbone is used through a beat block.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded watch dial. Recognisable engraving.
