# Ecommerce-jewelry — Motion vocabulary

> Default is locked-off static; pick at most one motion per 8s clip.

## Approved camera motions
- slow orbit around the hero piece (8-15 degrees, ease in/out)
- single rack focus from setting to centre stone
- macro push-in along a chain or band
- gentle tilt-down to reveal an engraving as the piece rotates

## Approved subject micro-motions
- ring rotates 30-45 degrees on a turntable, light catches a facet
- chain settles into place across velvet, links resolve cleanly
- earring sways once on its hook, no second oscillation
- gem catches a single sparkle as light tracks across it

## Pacing
- One primary motion per clip; ease-in / ease-out throughout.
- Hold the final frame for at least 1 second so sparkle reads.
- Match light tracking speed to the orbit speed.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, handheld shake, fast tracking.
- Multiple facets sparkling in unison (reads as CG fake).
- Pieces dropping onto the surface from above.
