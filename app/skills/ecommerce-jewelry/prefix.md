# Ecommerce-jewelry skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Editorial macro jewellery photography. Hard key light from the side to
strike facets and create sparkle, with a soft fill to keep the metal warm.
A subtle volumetric rim light traces the silhouette without overexposing.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static macro frame on the hero piece. Allowed motion is a single
slow orbit (8-15 degrees) or a single rack focus from setting to gem.
Prefer extreme close-up shot framing on the centre stone or pavé band.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named piece (yellow-gold solitaire ring, halo earring,
diamond pendant); secondary subject is the velvet or matte stone surface
beneath. Hold the same prop set across consecutive scenes within a beat.

## Audio
Audio: a soft single jewellery-box click, low fabric brushes, a quiet ring
of metal on stone. No music unless the scene asks for it.

## Constraints specific to jewellery
Faceted gem reflections must be physically plausible — no fake CG sparkle,
no melting metal, no real engraving from a recognisable brand.
