# Ecommerce-jewelry — Scene template

> Each scene = one ~8s Veo3 clip; locked-off static macro by default.

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip jewellery ad)

| Beat       | Goal                                       | Typical shot |
|------------|--------------------------------------------|--------------|
| reveal     | hero piece on velvet, light strikes facets | extreme close-up |
| detail     | named feature: setting, halo, prong        | macro single-stone |
| try-on     | piece on hand, ear, or neck                | macro with anchored body part |
| payoff     | final hero, light catches and resolves     | extreme close-up |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Backdrop fixed per beat block (deep velvet, ivory matte, warm wood).
- One specular highlight per facet — not a chandelier of fake stars.
- Negative space around the piece for stat cards / lower-thirds later.
- Stone, metal, and surface textures must read crisp and physical.

## Continuity across the block

- Same backdrop, same key-light direction, same macro DOF bokeh shape.
- Pieces in matching collections kept side-by-side when revealed.
