# Ecommerce-tech — Character template

> Template used by the adapter to describe a presenter or hands shown with
> the tech product. Keep faces neutral and non-identifiable (no real persons).
> Tech reviews often use hands-only or minimal presenter framing.

## Identity anchor (hard pin — reused across every scene)

- **Hands**: clean, well-groomed, no nail polish unless product targets that demographic
- **Wardrobe**: dark neutral top (black or charcoal) to avoid color distraction from product
- **Expression**: focused, professional, no exaggerated reactions
- **Framing**: hands-only for interaction shots; bust-up for presenter commentary

## Framing defaults

- Hands-only framing when demonstrating product features (ports, buttons, screen)
- Bust-up framing for presenter commentary scenes
- Product always in sharp focus; presenter slightly soft when both in frame
- Avoid covering product branding or key design elements

## Hard constraints

- No real identifiable persons
- No brand logos other than the featured product
- Hands must stay consistent across all scenes (same skin tone, same wardrobe)
- Product must remain the visual hero in every shot
