# Ecommerce-tech — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for precise, controlled product motion. Combine at
> most 2–3 micro-motions per scene. Camera stays locked-off (see `_base`).

## Product-only motions (no hands)

- slow 360° turntable rotation to reveal all sides
- slow Ken Burns push-in on detail (port, logo, texture)
- soft parallax drift across the product surface
- rack focus from overall silhouette to specific feature
- gentle tilt to catch rim light on edge

## Hands-interaction motions

- smooth plug-in of cable into port
- deliberate button press with finger
- screen swipe revealing UI
- product lift and rotate to show back panel
- unboxing lift from packaging

## Pacing

- One primary action per clip; resolve it within the 8s
- Ease-in / ease-out — no abrupt starts or stops
- Match motion energy to the beat (hook = dramatic reveal, detail = slow and precise)

## Avoid

- Fast whip motion, jump cuts, strobing
- Camera shake, handheld bob
- More than one major action per clip
- Fingerprints or smudges appearing during interaction shots
