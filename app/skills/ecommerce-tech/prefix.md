# Ecommerce-tech skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Clean studio tech product photography. Cool-white key light with
controlled rim lighting for sharp edge definition; soft fill to
eliminate harsh shadows. Photoreal, precision-engineering grade.
Macro detail shots highlight ports, buttons, and surface finish.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static frame at three-quarter or front-on. Allowed motion
vocabulary: slow 360-degree turntable rotation, slow Ken Burns
push-in on detail, soft parallax drift, single rack focus from
overall silhouette to a specific feature. Macro shot framing for
ports and buttons; wide for full product silhouette.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named device. Hands-only when interaction is shown
(see ``character.md``); product is always the visual hero. The
product chassis is graphite or brushed aluminium; the accent color
belongs to the screen content or a single rim highlight.

## Audio
Audio: clean studio room tone, controlled mechanical click on the
turntable lock, optional subtle synth swell on the reveal beat.
Dialogue is rare; if used, a single short tagline.

## Constraints specific to ecommerce-tech
No real brand markings. No fingerprints, dust, or scratches visible
during interaction shots. No melting CG materials, no glitch
artefacts. No real identifiable persons.
