# Experimental-abstract — Character template

## Default subject
- A non-human abstract phenomenon (liquid metal swirl, neon ink, chrome
  geometry, particle field bloom).
- No human form anywhere in the frame.

## Identity anchors
- Same phenomenon across the beat block.
- Same colour field; do not jump between palettes mid-block.

## Forbidden
- Human form of any kind. Real identifiable persons.
- Real product references.
