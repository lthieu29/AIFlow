# Experimental-abstract — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow continuous orbit around the abstract subject
- locked-off macro on a texture detail
- rack focus from foreground particle to background field

## Approved subject motions
- liquid metal swirls in a single continuous flow
- neon ink diffuses across water in a bloom
- chrome shape flexes once and holds
- a particle field blooms outward from a single point

## Pacing
- One phenomenon per clip; let it breathe.
- Synth pluck rhythm consistent across the block.
- Hold the closing frame for at least 1 second.

## Avoid
- Strobe / epilepsy-trigger flashes.
- Multiple phenomena competing in one shot.
- Human form anywhere in the frame.
