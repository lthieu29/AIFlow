# Experimental-abstract skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Abstract motion-design aesthetic. Bold synthwave colour fields, neon
edge-light, fluid liquid-metal CG-look (still rendered photoreal). Volumetric
beam light from a single source. Minimalist composition with negative
space.

## Camera framing
Slow continuous orbit around the abstract subject; locked-off macro on
texture; rack focus from foreground particle to background field. Shot
framing alternates between extreme close-up on texture and wide of the
abstract field.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single abstract phenomenon (liquid metal swirl, neon ink
diffusion, chrome shape flexing, particle field bloom). The phenomenon
is the subject; no humans, no products. Hold the same phenomenon
across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: a deep synth drone, one synth pluck per beat, glassy reverb
tail, slow modulating low-frequency oscillator. No dialogue.

## Constraints specific to abstract
No real identifiable persons (no human form at all). No real branded
context. No epilepsy strobe. No anime / cartoon stylisation.
