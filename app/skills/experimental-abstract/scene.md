# Experimental-abstract — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip experimental loop)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| open       | wide of the abstract field, slow orbit begins     | wide orbit |
| reveal     | macro on a texture detail (ink, metal, particle)  | extreme close-up |
| close      | pull-back to wide, phenomenon resolves            | wide orbit |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One synthwave colour field chosen per block.
- Negative space at top of frame for lyric / quote in post.
- No human, no product, no real-world context.

## Continuity across the block

- Same phenomenon, same colour field, same audio drone.
- Synth pluck rhythm aligned across cuts.
