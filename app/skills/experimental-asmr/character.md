# Experimental-asmr — Character template

## Default subject
- A hand interacting with a tactile surface; the hand and the surface
  are the only subjects.
- Optional ear-shape silhouette in soft focus.

## Identity anchors
- Same hand and same trigger surface across the block.
- Same lighting block.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Mouth or face-on-camera shots.
- Real branded packaging on triggers.
- Loud transient sounds.
