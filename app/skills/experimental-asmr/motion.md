# Experimental-asmr — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- locked-off extreme close-up
- very slow dolly-in toward the trigger source
- single rack focus from foreground texture to background

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a hand crinkles a single piece of paper once
- a brush sweeps across a microphone in one stroke
- a knife slices a soap block in a single cut
- a single rain drop strikes a leaf

## Pacing
- One ASMR trigger per clip; resolve cleanly.
- Hold the close-of-action frame for at least 2 seconds.
- Audio trigger isolated; no music bed.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cuts.
- Loud transients (claps, slams, glass shatter).
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashes.
