# Experimental-asmr skill prefix

## Style and lighting
ASMR sensory aesthetic. Warm soft tungsten key, halation on wet
surfaces, low contrast, photoreal macro. Emphasis on tactile texture
and intimate distance.

## Camera framing
Locked-off extreme close-up on the action surface. Allowed motion: a
very slow dolly-in toward the source, or a single rack focus from
foreground texture to background. Shot framing locked at extreme
close-up.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named ASMR action (a hand crinkling paper, a
brush sweeping across a microphone, a knife slicing a soap block,
a rain drop on a leaf). Face always off-frame; hands or props only.

## Audio
Audio: ASMR-grade isolated foley layered carefully — paper crinkle,
soft brush sweep, soap squelch, water drip, ear-to-ear directional
trigger sounds. Optional Dialogue: a single soft whisper line.

## Constraints specific to ASMR
No real identifiable face. No real branded packaging on triggers. No
loud transients (claps, slams, glass shatter). No epilepsy strobe.
