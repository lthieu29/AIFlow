# Experimental-asmr — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip ASMR loop)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| approach   | hand approaches the trigger object                | extreme close-up |
| trigger    | the named ASMR action lands cleanly               | extreme close-up |
| release    | resonance lingers, frame holds                    | extreme close-up |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One ASMR trigger per clip; do not chain triggers in one shot.
- Negative space at top of frame for caption / lyric in post.
- Specular highlights kept gentle to avoid bloom.

## Continuity across the block

- Same trigger surface across the block.
- Audio trigger isolated; no overlapping triggers.
