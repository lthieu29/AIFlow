# Explainer-finance — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- locked-off medium close-up on the presenter
- single slow push-in on the chart at the key data beat
- locked-off extreme close-up on a number / arrow

## Approved subject micro-motions
- presenter gestures once toward the chart behind
- a single chart element animates (bar grows, arrow points)
- a number ticks over once (no continuous counter)
- a single rack focus from presenter to chart

## Pacing
- One educational beat per clip.
- Hold the chart frame for at least 1.5 seconds.
- News-bed pad continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cutaways.
- Multiple charts animating simultaneously.
- Score swelling that overpowers the dialogue.
