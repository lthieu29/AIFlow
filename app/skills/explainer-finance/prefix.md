# Explainer-finance skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Newsroom-grade finance explainer. Bright cool key light from above-front,
soft fill from a paper bounce, dark navy or charcoal backdrop with a
glowing ticker-board behind. Photoreal news-grade detail.

## Camera framing
Locked-off medium close-up on the presenter; locked-off extreme close-up
on chart elements during cutaways. Allowed motion: a single slow push-in
on the chart at the key data beat, otherwise locked.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named presenter (analyst in dark blazer, professor in
soft sweater). Hold wardrobe across the beat block. Charts and tickers
stay on the same backdrop screen across consecutive scenes.

## Audio
Audio: clean studio room tone, soft data-board ticker pulse, optional
news-bed pad under wide cutaways. Dialogue: clear, measured tempo,
no jargon-stuffing.

## Constraints specific to finance
No real ticker symbols of public companies. No real bank / brokerage
branding. No specific buy / sell / price advice. No medical or legal
advice tone.
