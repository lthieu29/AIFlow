# Explainer-history — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow dolly across a display case
- single rack focus from artefact to scholar's hand
- locked-off medium of a period re-enactment
- single tilt-down to follow a quill onto parchment

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a quill writes a single line, then pauses
- a parchment page turns once
- a candle flame wavers on a re-enactment table
- an artefact rotates 30-45 degrees on a museum turntable

## Pacing
- One scholarly beat per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the artefact frame for at least 2 seconds.
- Cello pad continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, drone shots.
- Multiple artefacts animating at once.
- Anachronistic camera language (TikTok-style cuts).
