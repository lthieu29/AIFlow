# Explainer-history skill prefix

## Style and lighting
History documentary explainer. Sepia archive grain on cutaways, soft
gallery key light on artefacts, candle-and-window blend on period
re-enactments. Photoreal museum-grade detail.

## Camera framing
Slow dolly across an artefact on a glass display case; locked-off
medium of a period re-enactment; rack focus from a parchment to the
hand of a scholar. Shot framing alternates between extreme close-up
on objects and medium on re-enacted figures.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named historical subject (a Roman silver denarius, a
medieval letter, a Tang-dynasty bronze mirror) and the scholar / actor
who interprets it. Hold the artefact identity across the block.

## Audio
Audio: low scholarly room tone, soft cello pad, single quill scratch
on parchment, distant museum hush. Dialogue: measured narrative voice,
period-appropriate naming.

## Constraints specific to history
No real identifiable face for re-enactment actors. No anachronistic
props. No modern slang in dialogue. No nationalist propaganda framing.
