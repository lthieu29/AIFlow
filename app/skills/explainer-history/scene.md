# Explainer-history — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip history explainer)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| artefact   | display-case dolly past the named object          | extreme close-up |
| context   | scholar interprets, gloves the artefact          | medium close-up |
| reenact    | period re-enactment of the artefact's era        | medium static |
| conclude   | display-case lock, narrative takeaway            | extreme close-up |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Sepia / archive grain reserved for re-enactment cutaways.
- Gallery and re-enactment colour palettes kept distinct.
- Low-key shadow contrast across all scenes.

## Continuity across the block

- Same artefact across the gallery scenes.
- Same re-enactor wardrobe across the re-enactment block.
