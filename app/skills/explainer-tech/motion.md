# Explainer-tech — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for clean, purposeful tech explainer movement.
> Camera stays locked-off (manifest inherits camera_lock: true from _base).
> Combine at most 2–3 micro-motions per scene and resolve within the 8s clip.

## Presenter micro-motions

- deliberate point toward screen or diagram
- slow hand gesture tracing a concept flow
- slight forward lean to emphasise a key point
- confident nod while stating a conclusion
- turn from camera to screen, then back to camera

## Product and device motions

- slow 15–20° rotation to reveal device profile
- smooth screen-on animation — display lights up
- gentle tap or swipe gesture on touchscreen
- slow Ken Burns push-in on interface detail
- rack focus from device body to screen content

## Data and diagram motions

- elements appearing sequentially (left to right, top to bottom)
- progress bar or chart filling smoothly
- highlight pulse on a key data point
- smooth zoom into a specific section of a diagram

## Pacing

- One primary action per clip; resolve it cleanly within the 8s
- Ease-in / ease-out — deliberate and confident, never rushed
- Pause briefly on the key visual before transitioning
- Match motion energy to the beat: hook = dynamic entry, summary = settled hold

## Avoid

- Fast whip pans, jump cuts, or strobing effects
- Camera shake or handheld movement
- Cluttered simultaneous animations
- More than one major action per clip
