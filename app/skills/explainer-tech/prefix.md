# Explainer-tech skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Clean modern tech explainer aesthetic. Bright even studio lighting
with cool-white key light from above-front, soft diffused fill,
no harsh shadows. Photorealistic product renders and clean
interface mockups.

## Camera framing
Locked-off static frame at chest height for the presenter, eye-level
for desktops, top-down for mobile screens. Allowed motion: slow
Ken Burns push-in on a UI element, or a single rack focus from the
device body to its screen content. Tight close-ups for detail,
medium for presenter, wide for context.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a named presenter archetype (see ``character.md``) plus
the named device or interface (clean tech-casual wardrobe, no logos).
The product or interface is always the visual hero; the presenter
never obscures it.

## Audio
Dialogue: clear, paced explainer voice — one concept per beat.
Audio: clean studio room tone, soft data-board ticker pulse on
data beats, optional gentle synth pad under wide context shots.

## Constraints specific to explainer-tech
No real identifiable persons. No real brand logos or copyrighted
interface elements. No clutter, no harsh shadows, no vintage
aesthetics. Screens and interfaces must remain legible — avoid
glare, reflection, or rolling-shutter lines.
