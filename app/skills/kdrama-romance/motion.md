# K-Drama Romance — Motion vocabulary

> Approved motion phrases for emotional, cinematic drama beats. Camera motion
> is allowed for this skill (manifest sets `camera_lock: false`). Use at most
> 2–3 micro-motions per scene and resolve them within the 8s clip.

## Camera motions

- slow push-in on emotional beat
- gentle rack focus between two characters
- subtle handheld drift for tension
- slow dolly-out to reveal isolation
- match cut on eye contact

## Subject micro-motions

- hesitant step closer → pause → look away
- reach out → withdraw hand
- slow turn toward / away from the other character
- single tear welling, blink, breath
- hair brushed back, soft smile forming

## Slow-motion moments (turning points)

- wind-caught hair in golden light
- rain falling between characters
- the moment hands almost touch

## Pacing

- Ease-in / ease-out, lingering holds for emotion
- Let beats breathe — silence and stillness are valid
- Energy follows mood: tense = tighter, romantic = slower

## Avoid

- Fast action, whip pans, jump cuts
- Comedic or exaggerated movement
- Anime-style speed lines or cartoon physics
