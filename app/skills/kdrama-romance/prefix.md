# K-Drama Romance skill prefix

Cinematic Korean drama style. Soft diffused key light with warm golden tones.
Shallow depth of field, 50mm cinematic lens. Pastel color palette — soft pinks,
creams, and muted blues. Slow push-in camera movement for emotional beats.
Characters in medium close-up for dialogue scenes, extreme close-up for
emotional reactions. Gentle film grain, warm tone curve, soft vignette.
Romantic and bittersweet mood. Live-action drama series aesthetic.
No anime, no illustration, no cartoon style.
