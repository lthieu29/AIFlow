# K-Drama Romance skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Cinematic Korean live-action drama. Soft diffused key with warm
golden tones, blue-hour cool fill for melancholy beats, gentle
halation on highlights. 50mm cinematic lens, shallow depth of field,
subtle film grain and warm tone curve.

## Camera framing
Slow push-in on emotional beats; gentle rack focus between two
characters; subtle handheld drift only for tension; locked-off
medium for dialogue; extreme close-up for reaction.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named character set: lead, love interest, supporting
roles. Each character carries a hard identity anchor (see
``character.md``) — same wardrobe, hair, defining feature across
every scene and episode.

## Audio
Dialogue: tender, two-line maximum, tempered Korean-drama emotion.
Audio: soft piano theme, distant café ambience, autumn breeze, rain
on glass, single laugh fading; one bittersweet string phrase on
turning-point beats.

## Constraints specific to k-drama-romance
Live-action drama only — no anime, no illustration, no cartoon
stylisation. No real identifiable persons. No NSFW or explicit
intimacy. Keep identity consistent across all scenes and episodes.
