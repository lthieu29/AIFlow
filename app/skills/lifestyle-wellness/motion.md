# Lifestyle-wellness — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow dolly across a still life of a cup or yoga mat
- locked-off medium close-up on a meditative breath
- gentle tilt-down to feet on the mat at the close

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a single pour of matcha into a bowl
- a slow exhale, eyes closing
- one bare foot lifts to ground in a yoga pose
- a candle flame wavers once

## Pacing
- One ritual beat per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the close-of-ritual frame for 2 seconds.
- Audio drone continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cuts.
- Multi-pose flow sequences in one clip.
- Score swelling under the silence.
