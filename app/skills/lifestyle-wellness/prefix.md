# Lifestyle-wellness skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Calming wellness aesthetic. Soft sunrise key light through sheer
curtains, warm candle fill, matcha green and oat tones. Photoreal
short-form with subtle film grain.

## Camera framing
Slow dolly across a still life of a cup or yoga mat; locked-off medium
close-up on a meditative pose or breath; tilt-down to feet on a mat at
the close beat. Shot framing alternates between extreme close-up and
medium.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a named practitioner (hand pouring tea, bare feet on a
mat, a candle flame wavering). Face is partial or off-frame; the
ritual is the hero. Hold the same wardrobe and prop set across the
block.

## Audio
Audio: tibetan singing bowl drone, soft fabric brush, one teapot
pour, distant birdsong. Dialogue: a single calm intention line
spoken slowly.

## Constraints specific to wellness
No real identifiable face. No real branded apparel. No weight-loss
before / after framing. No extreme contortion poses.
