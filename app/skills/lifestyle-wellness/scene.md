# Lifestyle-wellness — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip wellness reel)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| awaken     | wide of bedroom at sunrise, light enters          | wide static |
| ritual     | dolly across a teapot pour or matcha bowl         | extreme close-up |
| practice   | locked-off medium of a yoga pose                  | medium close-up |
| close      | tilt-down to bare feet on the mat                 | medium static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named ritual prop per scene (teapot, candle, mat, journal).
- Misted morning haze in every shot.
- Negative space top of frame for caption / quote in post.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same mat, same prop set, same hour.
- Audio drone continuous across cuts.
