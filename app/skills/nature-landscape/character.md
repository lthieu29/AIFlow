# Nature-landscape — Character template

## Default subject
- The land itself: a sandstone canyon, a glacial valley, a paddy terrace,
  an autumn forest, a coastline.
- No human subjects — landscape only.

## Identity anchors
- Same location across the beat block.
- Same hour-of-day; do not jump from sunset to noon between clips.

## Forbidden
- Hikers, climbers, locals visible in frame. Vehicles, drones, signposts.
  Real identifiable persons of any kind.
