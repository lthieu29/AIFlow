# Nature-landscape — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- slow drone push-in across a wide vista
- slow drone pull-back from a feature
- locked-off wide static of a mountain face / waterfall
- single tilt-up to reveal sky from a foreground feature

## Approved subject micro-motions
- clouds drift (sped-up time-lapse implied) once
- water flows steadily over rocks
- grass or leaves bend in a single gust of wind
- shadow line tracks slowly across a face

## Pacing
- One drone move per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the lock at end for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Match drone speed to the orchestral phrase.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast zooms, drone yaw spins.
- Multiple environmental motions competing.
- Drone shake or visible propeller flicker.
