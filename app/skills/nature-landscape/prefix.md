# Nature-landscape skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Sweeping documentary landscape photography. Golden-hour key with rim
light from low sun, blue-hour cool fill across distant mountains, soft
volumetric shafts through cloud breaks. Photoreal, planet-Earth grade
of detail.

## Camera framing
Slow drone push-in across a wide vista, single tilt-up to reveal sky,
or a locked-off wide static of a mountain face. Shot framing leans
extreme wide and wide; medium only for foreground texture.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named landscape feature: a sandstone canyon, a glacial
valley, a paddy terrace, an autumn forest, a coastline. Hold the same
location and hour-of-day across the beat block.

## Audio
Audio: wind through grass, distant bird calls, water moving over stone,
a sustained orchestral pad on the wide reveal, hushed close-mic foley
on foreground texture.

## Constraints specific to landscape
No people in frame. No drone propeller flickers visible. No CG-looking
godrays, no plastic foliage. No real identifiable persons.
