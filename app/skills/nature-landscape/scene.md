# Nature-landscape — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip nature reel)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| reveal     | drone push-in across the vista, sun rises         | extreme wide drone |
| feature    | locked-off wide of the named feature              | wide static |
| texture    | medium of foreground (grass, water, rock, leaves) | medium static |
| payoff     | drone pull-back, sky fills the frame              | extreme wide drone |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named hour-of-day per block (golden hour, blue hour, overcast).
- Sky and land split close to rule of thirds.
- Foreground texture in mediums; never crowd the wide shots.

## Continuity across the block

- Same location, same hour, same wind direction.
- Audio (wind / water) sustained across cuts.
