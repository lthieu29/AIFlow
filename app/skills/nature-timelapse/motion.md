# Nature-timelapse — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- absolutely none — camera is locked

## Approved subject motions
- clouds drift across the sky in time-lapse cadence
- shadows track across a landscape feature
- light shifts hour-of-day in the same frame
- distant water cycles colour as the sun sets

## Pacing
- One full hour-arc per clip.
- Match the speed of cloud drift across cuts so the impression is
  cinematic and not jittery.
- Audio pad swells to the brightest light moment.

## Avoid
- Camera motion of any kind.
- Birds flying past close to camera.
- Vehicles in frame.
- Sky stitched from multiple takes that do not match cloud direction.
