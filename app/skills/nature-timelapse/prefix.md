# Nature-timelapse skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Documentary time-lapse landscape photography. Naturalistic light cycling
across hours: blue hour to golden hour to noon to dusk. Photoreal, no
glow effects, no fake godrays. Camera absolutely fixed throughout.

## Camera framing
Absolutely locked-off wide static. The frame never moves. Allowed shot
framing is wide or extreme wide; no zoom, no pan, no tilt during the
time-lapse.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named landscape (mountain range, beach, glacier, urban
skyline at night). Hold the same vantage across the entire block;
the only change is light and clouds.

## Audio
Audio: gentle ambient pad, sustained string drone, soft wind that swells
slowly. Optional Dialogue: one short naturalist line over the dawn
beat.

## Constraints specific to time-lapse
No people, no vehicles, no animals foregrounded. No camera motion of any
kind. No artificial colour shifts (e.g. neon-pink sky). No real
identifiable persons.
