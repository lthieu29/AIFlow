# Nature-timelapse — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip time-lapse cycle)

| Beat       | Goal                                            | Typical shot |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| dawn       | blue-hour starting frame, clouds drift in       | extreme wide static |
| morning    | golden-hour key, shadows shorten                | extreme wide static |
| midday     | overhead light, crisp shadows                   | extreme wide static |
| dusk       | sunset gold to twilight magenta, stars emerge   | extreme wide static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- Same vantage across all clips.
- One named landscape feature centred or on rule of thirds.
- No moving elements other than light and weather.

## Continuity across the block

- Lock vantage; lock focal length; lock frame.
- Audio pad continuous across cuts.
