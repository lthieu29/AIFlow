# Nature-wildlife — Character template

## Default subject
- A single named species (red panda, snow leopard, mandarin duck, dhole).
- The animal is healthy and observed at a respectful distance.

## Identity anchors
- Same individual across the beat block (markings consistent).
- Same habitat lighting block.

## Forbidden
- Captive zoo enclosures, fences, food bowls visible.
- Trophy or hunting context. Anthropomorphised gestures.
- Real identifiable persons in the frame.
