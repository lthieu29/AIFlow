# Nature-wildlife — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- long telephoto with a natural handheld sway
- slow rack focus from foliage to the animal
- locked-off medium when the animal is still
- a slow follow-pan as the animal walks parallel to the lens

## Approved subject micro-motions
- ear or tail flick once
- head turns toward the lens, eye contact for a beat
- single step forward, then halt
- slow chew, slow breath, slow blink

## Pacing
- One observation beat per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the eye-contact frame for at least 2 seconds.
- Match camera sway to the breath of the subject.

## Avoid
- Drones near wildlife, fast tracking, multi-animal chase.
- Score swelling over each call.
- Multiple species in the same shot competing for focus.
