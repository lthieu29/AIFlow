# Nature-wildlife skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Wildlife documentary photography. Naturalistic key light from sun, dappled
forest shafts, overcast soft fill where the canopy diffuses light.
Photoreal, planet-Earth-grade detail on fur, feather, scale.

## Camera framing
Long telephoto lens with a natural sway suggesting handheld observation.
Slow rack focus from foliage to the subject when the animal emerges. Shot
framing favours medium close-up of the subject and extreme close-up on
the eye, paw, or wing.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named species (red panda, snow leopard, mandarin
duck, dhole, white-tail deer). Hold the same individual across the beat;
do not switch animals mid-block.

## Audio
Audio: forest ambience, distant species calls, leaves rustling, single
heartbeat-like sub-bass on the close-up beat. Optional Dialogue: a calm
naturalist voiceover line.

## Constraints specific to wildlife
No captive zoo backdrop. No trophy / hunting context. No
anthropomorphised gestures. No real identifiable persons.
