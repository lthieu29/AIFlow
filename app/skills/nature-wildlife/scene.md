# Nature-wildlife — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip wildlife observation)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| habitat    | wide of the habitat at golden hour                | wide static |
| reveal     | rack focus from foliage to the subject            | medium close |
| behaviour  | named behaviour (drinking, grooming, calling)     | extreme close-up |
| return     | slow follow-pan as the animal walks off-frame     | medium static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named species per block.
- Habitat established once in the wide; do not re-establish.
- Eye-line of the animal kept toward camera-left or camera-right
  consistently.

## Continuity across the block

- Same individual across all clips.
- Same habitat lighting and time of day.
