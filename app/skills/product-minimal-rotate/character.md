# Product-minimal-rotate — Character template

> No human subjects. The product is the only character.

## Default subject
- One named product per shot, oriented at three-quarter angle.
- True product colours; no recolouring.
- Single optional accessory surface (matching cylinder or cube puck).

## Forbidden
- Hands, fingers, or any human limb in frame.
- Branded packaging visible behind the product.
- Multiple products in a comparison row.
