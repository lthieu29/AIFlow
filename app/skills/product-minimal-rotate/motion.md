# Product-minimal-rotate — Motion vocabulary

> Pure rotation; no other camera motion.

## Approved subject motions
- 30-degree rotation (reveal a profile change)
- 90-degree rotation (front to side)
- 360-degree rotation (full reveal, slow ease-out at the end)

## Approved camera motions
- absolutely none — camera is locked

## Pacing
- One rotation per clip.
- Ease-out so the rotation visibly slows before the lock.
- Hold the final frame for at least 1 second.

## Avoid
- Camera dolly, pan, tilt, or orbit.
- Wobble during rotation.
- Multiple rotation speeds within a single clip.
