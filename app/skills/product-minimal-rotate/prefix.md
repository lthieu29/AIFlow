# Product-minimal-rotate skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Pure minimal studio product turntable. Soft three-point key + rim + fill
on a seamless white or muted grey backdrop. Even, shadowless illumination
that flatters geometry without losing texture. Optional very soft drop
shadow under the product.

## Camera framing
Locked-off frame at three-quarter angle while the product rotates on a
turntable. Allowed motion is a single controlled rotation: 30, 90, or 360
degrees. Shot framing is medium hero with negative space all around.

## Subject anchoring
One product per shot. Anchor on the named item (matte ceramic mug,
brushed-steel kettle, leather wallet); no accessories or props beyond a
single optional surface puck.

## Audio
Audio: a low studio room tone, optional soft turntable click as it
locks. No music unless the scene asks for it.

## Constraints specific to minimal-rotate
No clutter, no hand reaching into frame, no environment props, no
gradient or coloured backdrop, no exaggerated reflections.
