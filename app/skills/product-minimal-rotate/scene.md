# Product-minimal-rotate — Scene template

> One product, one rotation, locked-off camera. Ideal for catalogue clips
> generated from a single still image via the `photo_slideshow` adapter.

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip catalogue)

| Beat       | Goal                                          | Typical shot |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------|
| reveal     | front-on with negative space, light settles   | medium hero, locked |
| profile    | 90 or 360 rotation showing all sides          | medium hero with rotation |
| payoff     | front-on locked, negative space for label     | medium hero, locked |

## Per-scene composition rules

- White or muted-grey seamless backdrop, picked once for the catalogue run.
- Negative space on at least one side of the frame for SKU lower-thirds.
- Optional very subtle drop shadow under the product.
- True colour; no flattering recolour.

## Continuity across the block

- Same backdrop, same lighting, same product orientation start.
- Rotation direction kept consistent across all clips in the catalogue.
