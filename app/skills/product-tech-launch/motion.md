# Product-tech-launch — Motion vocabulary

> Cinematic and controlled. Pick one combo motion per 8s clip.

## Approved camera motions
- slow controlled orbit (15-30 degrees, ease-in/out) with simultaneous
  dolly push-in toward the device
- single tilt-up from base to top of a tall device (phone standing
  upright, gaming tower)
- rack focus from foreground accent to hero device behind
- fixed extreme close-up with subtle parallax breathing

## Approved subject micro-motions
- screen lights up once, content slides in from below
- camera lens iris adjusts in a single beat (closing or opening)
- folding hinge opens to a fixed angle, holds
- device hovers off the surface a few centimetres, then settles

## Pacing
- One combo move per clip; resolve cleanly within 8s.
- Hold the final hero frame for at least 1.5 seconds for the tagline beat.
- Match orbit speed to the synth swell so the frame lock aligns with the
  music peak.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast tracking, handheld shake.
- Multiple devices entering the frame simultaneously.
- Glitch overlays, RGB shift, neon vapourwave overlays.
