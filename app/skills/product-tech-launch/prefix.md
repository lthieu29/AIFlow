# Product-tech-launch skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Apple-style hero launch photography. Hard key light from above-front, sharp
rim light on the metal edge, deep matte-black or graphite backdrop. Strong
specular highlights on glass and aluminium, no fingerprints visible.

## Camera framing
Combine a slow controlled orbit (15-30 degrees) with a synchronised dolly
push-in. Shot framing moves from wide hero (entire device against negative
space) to extreme close-up on the most distinctive feature: lens cluster,
fingerprint sensor, port cutout, fold seam.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the device: name it explicitly (slate-grey mirrorless camera,
folded aluminium phone, transparent gaming mouse). Hold proportions and
materials across consecutive scenes; one device per shot.

## Audio
Audio: a clean rising synth swell, a single mechanical click as the camera
locks the hero shot, low-mix studio room tone. Optional Dialogue: a single
short tagline line.

## Constraints specific to tech launch
No real brand logo, no model number visible, no leaked fingerprints, no
melting CG materials, no glitch artefacts.
