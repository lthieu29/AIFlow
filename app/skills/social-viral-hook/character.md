# Social-viral-hook — Character template

## Default subject
- A single hand, face partial, or hero object centred in frame.
- Bright clothing in saturated primaries (red, yellow, electric blue).
- Subject holds the hook prop steady across the 3-second hook.

## Identity anchors
- The face stays partial or off-frame; reveal is a hand or a lip-sync
  silhouette.
- Wardrobe stable through the hook beat.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Branded clothing. Other subjects in frame.
- Children unaccompanied; medical-looking content.
