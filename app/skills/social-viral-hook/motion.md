# Social-viral-hook — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- light handheld breathing (subtle)
- single whip-pan to land on the payoff
- locked-off final frame for the reveal

## Approved subject micro-motions
- hand approaches the prop in a single deliberate motion
- prop reveals (lid lifts, paper unwraps, cap snaps)
- subject mouths the hook line once and freezes
- light flickers once on the beat drop

## Pacing
- Hook lands in the first 1 second.
- Beat drop at 1.0s into clip 2.
- Final reveal frame held for 1 second before cutting.

## Avoid
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashing.
- Multiple competing motions in the hook second.
- Slow zen pacing — this skill is fast and bright.
