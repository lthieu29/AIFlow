# Social-viral-hook skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Bright social-first photography. Punchy daylight key with bounce fill,
saturated primary backgrounds, optional neon accent. Crisp clean
photoreal style with a vibrant TikTok colour grade.

## Camera framing
Light handheld breathing or a single quick whip-pan to land on the hook.
Fast cuts implied by tight medium and extreme close-up framing on the
subject's reaction or product. The first second always lands the hook.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named hook subject (a hand revealing a product, a
face reacting, a glass shattering). Hold the same wardrobe and prop
throughout the 3-second hook.

## Audio
Dialogue: a single 5-7 word hook line in the first second.
Audio: punchy bass drop on the reveal, a sharp ASMR cue (snap, pop,
unwrap), trending royalty-free beat.

## Constraints specific to social-viral-hook
No flashing strobe (epilepsy safety). No face-doxing. No misleading
clickbait copy. No real brand logos.
