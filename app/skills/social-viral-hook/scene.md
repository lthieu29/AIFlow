# Social-viral-hook — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip viral hook)

| Beat       | Goal                                                 | Typical shot |
|------------|------------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| hook       | first 3 seconds: question, gesture, or reveal        | medium close-up, handheld |
| build      | escalation: hand approaches the prop, beat drops     | extreme close-up, whip-pan |
| payoff     | reveal hits, subject reacts, freeze frame            | medium static, locked |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named saturated background colour per beat block.
- Subject centre-frame; rule of thirds reserved for the payoff frame.
- Negative space at top of frame for an emoji-style caption later.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same prop, same backdrop colour.
- Beat drop position consistent: 1.0s into clip 2 every time.
