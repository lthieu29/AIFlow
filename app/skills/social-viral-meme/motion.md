# Social-viral-meme — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- absolutely locked-off (the whole point is meme stillness)

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a single shrug
- a slow blink with a deadpan stare
- a printer prints a single page that flips out
- a phone notification flashes once

## Pacing
- The punchline lands inside 5 seconds.
- One reaction beat per clip; no multi-beat skits.

## Avoid
- Camera motion of any kind.
- Multiple punchlines in a single clip.
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashes.
