# Social-viral-meme skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Internet meme aesthetic. Flat sticker-bright colour, blocky shadows, low
DOF backdrop, bouncy practical light. Cheap-shot photoreal humour with
big readable composition (caption-friendly negative space at top).

## Camera framing
Locked-off medium static shot. Subject centred. Reserve negative space
at the top 25% for a caption to be added later in post (do NOT burn the
caption into the frame).

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single relatable scenario subject (a desk worker stares at
a printer, a cat sits on a keyboard, a person opens an empty fridge).
Hold the same prop and pose throughout the punchline beat.

## Audio
Audio: a sharp 'wahh wahh wahh' trombone loss cue or a synth swell hit;
optional one-line voiceover with a deadpan delivery; minimal ambient.

## Constraints specific to meme
No real identifiable face. No copyrighted meme template content. No
text overlay burned into the video (test enforces this). No mockery of
protected groups.
