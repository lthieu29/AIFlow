# Social-viral-meme — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 1-clip meme)

| Beat       | Goal                                                | Typical shot |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| setup      | relatable scenario, subject and prop in frame       | medium static |
| punch      | the relatable reaction, sound cue lands             | medium static (held) |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One backdrop colour, one prop, one reaction.
- Top 25% of frame reserved for caption space (added in post, not burned).
- No camera motion at all.

## Continuity across the block

- Single clip; no continuity needed within the meme itself.
- If chained into a 3-clip remix, keep prop, backdrop, lighting identical.
