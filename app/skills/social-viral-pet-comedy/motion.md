# Social-viral-pet-comedy — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- light handheld breathing on the human level
- single tilt-down to reveal the pet from owner's POV
- locked-off close-up on the pet's face

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a single ear flick
- one paw lifted, then placed
- a head-tilt confused look (no rapid head shaking)
- a single cat blink, slow
- a small bark / meow synced to the punchline cue

## Pacing
- Setup (human action) on clip 1.
- Pet reaction lands on clip 2 with a sound cue.
- Hold the reaction face for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Avoid
- Multiple pets in chaotic motion.
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashes.
- Distressed animals or training-collar moments.
