# Social-viral-pet-comedy skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Bright cheerful domestic interior. Soft warm window light + practical
lamp fill, slight halation on highlights. Photoreal pet detail with
cute, harmless humour aesthetic.

## Camera framing
Light handheld breathing or locked-off close-up on the pet's face. Tilt
down from human eye level to the pet's height for the reveal. Shot
framing favours close-up of the pet, medium of the human prop.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named pet (a sleepy tabby cat, a confused beagle, a
chinchilla on a perch). Held same wardrobe / collar / cage across the
beat. The human is hand-only or torso-only; no face.

## Audio
Audio: a single sharp 'huh?' synth cue at the punchline beat, soft
domestic ambience (kettle, distant TV, wood floor creak), pet vocal
overlay limited to one bark or one meow.

## Constraints specific to pet-comedy
No animal distress, no shock collars, no rapid scene swaps. No real
pet-owner faces. No copyrighted training brand visible.
