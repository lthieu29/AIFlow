# Social-viral-pet-comedy — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 3-clip pet comedy)

| Beat       | Goal                                         | Typical shot |
|------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------|
| setup      | human owner does something quirky            | medium static |
| reaction   | pet reacts: head-tilt / ear flick / paw lift | close-up |
| punch      | reaction held with sound cue / one-word jab  | close-up locked |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named cosy interior backdrop chosen per beat block.
- Pet at eye level for close-ups, never shot from above.
- Negative space at top of frame for caption text in post.

## Continuity across the block

- Same pet, same backdrop, same human prop.
- Sound cue arrives at exactly the same beat in clip 2.
