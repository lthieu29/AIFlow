# Social-viral-transform — Character template

## Default subject
- One transformation subject (room, hairstyle, outfit, plate, plant).
- A single optional human assistant — hand only, no face.
- Backdrop and lighting block fixed; subject is what changes.

## Identity anchors
- Same vantage point in before and after.
- Same lighting block; differences belong to the subject only.

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face. Medical-looking before/after of a body. Mid-cut
  introduction of new props that were not there before.
