# Social-viral-transform — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- locked-off medium hero before
- single whip-pan to the after vantage
- locked-off medium hero after

## Approved subject micro-motions
- a hand sweeps across the frame to hide the cut
- the lid of a box flips closed and reopens transformed
- fabric drops over the subject and falls away
- a sliding door closes and reopens

## Pacing
- Before clip held 2-3 seconds.
- Cut on the beat drop precisely.
- After clip held 2-3 seconds with a final freeze on reveal.

## Avoid
- Multiple cuts in the transition (one cut only).
- Strobe, epilepsy-trigger flashes.
- Mid-cut prop swaps that make the change implausible.
