# Social-viral-transform skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Bright transformation reveal aesthetic. Saturated daylight key with a
bounce fill on the after-shot side. The before-shot uses the same
lighting block to make the reveal honest. Photoreal short-form grade.

## Camera framing
Locked-off medium hero on the subject during the before. A single
whip-pan or match-on-action cut hides the transformation moment, then
locked-off medium hero again on the after. Mirror compositions help
the eye snap to the change.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on the named transformation subject (room, hairstyle, outfit,
table setting, plant, plate of food). The same vantage point is used
before and after so the change is unambiguous.

## Audio
Audio: a sharp suction or whoosh on the cut, a single bass drop on the
reveal, optional acapella vocal hit. Optional Dialogue: a one-word
reaction line (yes / wow / clean) on the after frame.

## Constraints specific to transform
No misleading photoshop tricks (overlay swaps, fake props introduced
mid-transition). No real face transformation that could imply medical
results. No epilepsy strobe.
