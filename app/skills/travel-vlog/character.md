# Travel-vlog — Character template

## Default subject
- A traveller in a linen shirt or hoodie, back-of-head walking shots
  only; never a full face.
- Local street vendors, monks, market sellers in soft focus.

## Identity anchors
- Same wardrobe and bag across all clips of the trip.
- Local subjects kept anonymous (back, hands, partial side).

## Forbidden
- Real identifiable face for traveller or locals.
- Real branded hotel / restaurant signage in clear focus.
- Drone shots over restricted cultural sites.
