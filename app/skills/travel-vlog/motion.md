# Travel-vlog — Motion vocabulary

## Approved camera motions
- light handheld breathing on walking shots
- locked-off wide of a named landmark
- single rack focus from market foreground to skyline
- gentle drone push-in across a permitted vista

## Approved subject micro-motions
- traveller takes one slow step into the market
- a vendor stirs a pot once
- a paper map flutters in a breeze
- a dog walks past once in the background

## Pacing
- One environmental beat per clip; ease throughout.
- Hold the locked landmark frame for at least 1.5 seconds.
- Audio bed (market / wind / temple bell) continuous across cuts.

## Avoid
- Whip pans, fast cutaways.
- Drone over restricted cultural sites.
- Music swelling over every line.
