# Travel-vlog skill prefix

## Style and lighting
Travel vlog cinematography. Naturalistic golden-hour key, warm sun
through narrow alleys, blue-hour silhouette on the skyline. Photoreal,
travel-magazine-grade colour with a single warm accent.

## Camera framing
Light handheld breathing on walking shots; locked-off wide of named
landmarks; single rack focus from foreground street life to skyline.
Shot framing alternates between extreme wide for vista and medium
close-up for street vendor / local detail.

## Subject anchoring
Anchor on a single named traveller (back-of-head walking shots in
linen shirt, a hand holding a paper map). The traveller does not show
their full face. Local subjects on the street stay in soft focus.

## Audio
Audio: distant prayer call or temple bell, market chatter, the splash
of a fountain, a street musician's flute, a single laugh. Dialogue:
casual one-line voiceover.

## Constraints specific to travel
No real identifiable face for the traveller or for locals. No real
restaurant / hotel branding visible. No drone shots over cultural
sites where prohibited.
