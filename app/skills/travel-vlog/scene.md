# Travel-vlog — Scene template

## Beat skeleton (typical 4-clip travel reel)

| Beat       | Goal                                              | Typical shot |
|------------|---------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| arrival    | wide of named city / landmark, golden hour        | wide static |
| street     | walking handheld through a market alley           | medium handheld |
| detail     | medium close-up of local detail (food, fabric)    | medium close-up |
| skyline    | blue-hour silhouette, traveller back-of-head      | wide static |

## Per-scene composition rules

- One named city / landmark per block; do not jump cities.
- Local subjects in soft focus; never sharp on a full face.
- Audio bed (market / wind / temple bell) continuous across cuts.

## Continuity across the block

- Same wardrobe, same bag, same hour-of-day window.
- Light direction consistent across consecutive scenes.
